Quick Firefly Facts

  • Fireflies are beetles (family Lampyridae), not flies.
  • Their glow comes from a chemical reaction called bioluminescence.
  • Flash patterns help fireflies find mates of their own species.
  • Many species are active at dusk and in warm, humid weather.
  • Some firefly populations are declining due to habitat loss and light pollution.

Safety Rules

  • Use a flashlight with red filter and keep brightness low.
  • Wear closed-toe shoes and long sleeves in tall grass.
  • Watch your step near water, roots, and uneven trails.
  • Use insect repellent carefully and follow label directions.
  • Do not capture or disturb wildlife in protected areas.

Best Viewing Tips

  • Go out 20–60 minutes after sunset.
  • Choose dark places away from streetlights and headlights.
  • Wait quietly for your eyes to adjust for 10–15 minutes.
  • Look near meadow edges, shrubs, and damp areas.
  • Turn off porch/phone lights to improve visibility.
